## Selection rule

The resolver starts from a capability vacancy, checks native coverage and the
employee's exact ref, then filters by qualification, permissions, host support,
checker independence, context/runtime cost, and maintenance. Stars and
aggregate quality scores are not used. A `QUARANTINED` or floating-ref employee
cannot be selected; a `SPECIALIST` or `PROVISIONAL` employee remains visibly
conditional and must emit a `delegation_plan.json` plus `handoff.json`.
