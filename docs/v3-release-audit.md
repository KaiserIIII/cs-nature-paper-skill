# V3 release audit record

Audit date: 2026-08-27. These are three role-separated audit passes performed
locally; they record residual risk and are not a claim that the skill is
scientifically infallible or independently validated by another model.

## Round 1 - Research scientist

- **Question:** Can the workflow induce fabricated science, overclaiming,
  meaningless experiments, or false novelty?
- **Controls checked:** claim/evidence anchors, source-support relation,
  closest-work adversary, falsifiers, feasibility gate, decision matrix,
  discovery/pilot/formal labels, amendment preservation, and no reviewer votes.
- **Result:** PASS for the represented invariants. Residual risk: the host/model
  can still misunderstand a domain profile; formal projects require a qualified
  methods checker and project-specific trials.

## Round 2 - Agent architect

- **Question:** Are routing, context, tools, state, external skills, and
  progressive disclosure coherent?
- **Controls checked:** graph schema and append-only events, V2 migration,
  vacancy-first employee registry, permissions/budget, host adapters, and
  context-budget routing.
- **Result:** PASS for local scripts and schemas. Residual risk: external
  connector behavior and current venue pages are environment-dependent and must
  be re-verified at activation time.

## Round 3 - Student user

- **Question:** Can a capable but inexperienced undergraduate use the system
  without being buried in jargon or forced to make avoidable decisions?
- **Controls checked:** Autopilot sequence, compact mentoring contract,
  recommended defaults, feasibility alternatives, progress summary, and only
  material author checkpoints.
- **Result:** PASS for the documented UX contract. Residual risk: explanations
  remain model-generated; a real pilot with representative students is needed
  before claiming usability evidence.

## Release disposition

The local candidate passes unit tests, schema checks, quick validation, and
behavior-case coverage checks. Full model/harness trials from
`docs/behavior-evaluation.md` remain a release-owner action before claiming
behavioral evidence. Do not merge to `main`, publish artifacts, connect
accounts, or submit papers automatically. Requalify external employees and
re-run affected pressure cases whenever their exact pin changes.
