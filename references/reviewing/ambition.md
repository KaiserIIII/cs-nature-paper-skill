# Ambition analyzer

`scripts/ambition_analyzer.py` reports one of `READY`, `PLAUSIBLE_WITH_GAPS`,
or `NOT_YET_DEFENSIBLE` for levels A (coursework) through E (broad high-impact
ambition). It explains ten dimensions separately: importance, novelty depth,
mechanistic insight, technical depth, generality, evidence diversity,
robustness, reproducibility, external relevance, and audience breadth. It never
produces an acceptance probability or treats a marketing label as evidence.
