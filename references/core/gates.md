# Gate contracts

## Argument gate

Require problem/stakeholder, phenomenon or artifact, prior knowledge, gap,
mechanism, constructs with operationalization, population/scope, RQs,
contribution, falsifiers, alternatives, and downstream boundaries.

## Evidence and protocol gates

Require a claim/evidence matrix, denominators, estimands, missingness,
dependence, multiplicity, stopping/failure rules, frozen inputs, and an
append-only amendment path. Discovery and pilot output cannot be relabeled as
formal evidence.

## Feasibility gate

Return `GO`, `GO_WITH_SCOPE_REDUCTION`, `PILOT_FIRST`, `HIGH_RISK`, or `NO_GO`.
For `NO_GO`, propose a lower-resource design that preserves the scientific
question or explain why no defensible reduction exists.

## Submission and completion gates

Verify current primary venue sources, template, article type, anonymity,
declarations, data/code policy, AI disclosure, page rules, and package hashes.
Use `PASS`, `CONDITIONAL`, or `FAIL` with exact anchors. A producer report is
not independent verification.
