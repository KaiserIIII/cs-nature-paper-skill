# Publication Sufficiency

V4 evaluates five ordered gates without collapsing them into one score:

1. Scientific Validity: the bounded claims follow from the design and evidence.
2. Evidence Sufficiency: load-bearing claims have observed, traceable evidence.
3. Publication Sufficiency: the study has enough dataset, model, baseline,
   ablation, mechanism, external-validation, literature, and manuscript depth.
4. Reviewer Completeness: novelty, domain, methods, statistics, experimental
   completeness, reproducibility, and adversarial reviewers have tried to kill
   the paper and unresolved major concerns are recorded.
5. Submission Readiness: every preceding gate passes and the Publication Editor
   records a realistic target and remaining author actions.

Strong evidence for a narrow claim is not automatically sufficient for a strong
paper. Repeated seeds, shifts, or jobs improve uncertainty estimates but do not
add research breadth. When a depth dimension fails, use the Research Expansion
Planner before manuscript polish. No gate predicts acceptance or authorizes
submission.
