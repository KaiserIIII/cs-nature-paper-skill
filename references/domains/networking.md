# Domain profile: networking

- Contributions: protocol, routing, congestion control, measurement, or networked service.
- RQs/evidence: goodput, latency/tail, loss, fairness, energy, resilience, or deployment behavior under named topology/workload.
- Baselines: standard protocol and closest modern alternative with matched link rates, buffers, and traffic.
- Threats: simulator realism, trace selection, topology bias, clock/measurement error, hidden queueing, unfair tuning.
- Experiments/statistics: controlled topology/traffic matrix, sensitivity and failure behavior where claims require them.
- Artifacts/reviewer focus: traces, simulator/version or testbed, packet capture, config, license, reproducible scripts.
- Fatal mistakes: simulator-only deployment claims, cherry-picked traces, mean latency without tail or loss context.
- Specialists: network measurement, distributed systems, security and privacy.
