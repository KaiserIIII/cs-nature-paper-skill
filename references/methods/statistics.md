# Statistics and uncertainty routing

Choose analysis from the estimand, unit, outcome, dependence, sampling frame,
and missingness in the frozen protocol. A domain profile can suggest a method;
it cannot decide one without the study design.

- Report the estimate, uncertainty interval, denominator, exclusions, and
  effect size when an inferential comparison is made.
- Model clustering, repeated measures, paired outcomes, and unequal exposure;
  do not treat rows as independent by default.
- Register transformations, stopping rules, multiplicity, and exploratory
  versus confirmatory status before formal analysis.
- Inspect raw distributions and diagnostics; when assumptions fail, use a
  defensible alternative or report sensitivity rather than silently deleting
  observations.
- A null result is informative only relative to precision and the decision it
  would change. A p-value is not a mechanism, practical importance, or proof of
  causality.
- Bayesian, bootstrap, permutation, robust, or non-parametric methods are
  options when their assumptions and interpretation are stated.

The statistical checker verifies that the analysis instrument matches the claim;
it does not certify construct validity or replace a domain methods review.
