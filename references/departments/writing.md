# Writing department contract

- **Mission:** communicate the evidence-bound argument to the intended editor and reader.
- **Trigger:** argument and evidence anchors exist, or a revision concern needs prose repair.
- **Required inputs:** contract, claims, verified source matrix, formal results, figures, venue card.
- **Required capabilities:** evidence-bound drafting, claim verification, editorial compression, document engineering.
- **Optional capabilities:** bilingual style, humanization, response-letter specialist.
- **Producer/checker:** drafter and claim/number checker; preserve confidential correspondence locally.
- **Allowed tools:** approved LaTeX/document tools and current primary venue sources.
- **Forbidden:** unsupported novelty, fabricated citations/numbers, AI-disclosure concealment, automatic submission.
- **Output contract:** title/abstract/intro/method/results/discussion, supplement, declarations, claim traces.
- **Evidence contract:** each load-bearing sentence links to a claim and anchor; limitations and downstream boundaries remain.
- **Handoff:** compiled package, source tree, manifest, unresolved author decisions to validation.
- **Failure:** broken references, scope inflation, stale venue rules, inconsistent terminology or numbers.
- **Stop:** editor answers the 90-second questions and further edits are stylistic rather than corrective.
- **Reopen:** review finding, evidence update, venue change, or validation mismatch.
- **Student explanation:** writing is the final expression of the argument, not a place to promise results before they exist.
