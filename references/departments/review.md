# Review department contract

- **Mission:** expose the strongest credible objection and the smallest defensible repair.
- **Trigger:** validation is pass/conditional or a draft needs diagnosis.
- **Required inputs:** current manuscript, claims, evidence map, venue/domain/study profile, known risks.
- **Required capabilities:** editor, closest competitor, methods/theory, artifact, ethics, adversarial and student review as relevant.
- **Optional capabilities:** pragmatist, historian, security or deployment specialist.
- **Producer/checker:** independent perspectives when available; otherwise label them as one agent's perspectives.
- **Allowed tools:** local draft, verified sources, checkable artifacts, current venue rules.
- **Forbidden:** reviewer voting, acceptance probability, manufactured independence, unanchored consensus.
- **Output contract:** finding ID, severity, anchor, violated claim/construct, alternative, smallest fix, data need, residual risk, disagreement.
- **Evidence contract:** findings point to manuscript/artifact lines and distinguish missing information from contradiction.
- **Handoff:** targeted reopen/amendment to innovation, experiment, writing, or validation.
- **Failure:** role lacks evidence, conflict is unresolved, private review leaks, or proposed fix changes scope silently.
- **Stop:** threats are covered, critical findings resolved or author-accepted with bounded claims, and no new decision-relevant issue appears.
- **Reopen:** new evidence, reviewer concern, or venue/target change.
- **Student explanation:** review is a stress test of claims, not a popularity contest or promise of acceptance.
