# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — 2026-08-26

### Changed

- Replaced the mandatory end-to-end pipeline with seven task modes and six
  evidence-based gates.
- Reframed orchestration around claim, evidence, mechanism, construct scope,
  falsifiers, and decision-changing experiments.
- Made the seven departments adaptive roles rather than a fixed sequence.
- Replaced static venue tables with a live primary-source verification protocol.
- Replaced reviewer vote counts and acceptance theater with severity, evidence
  anchors, disagreement synthesis, and bounded stopping conditions.
- Added rejection recovery that freezes the submitted state, protects private
  correspondence, and registers post-decision amendments.
- Added explicit prerequisite/downstream boundaries for constructs such as
  dependency resolution and reproducibility.

### Added

- Private `.research-state/` templates for a research contract, evidence ledger,
  and decision log.
- A dependency-free `research_state.py` initializer and four gate audits.
- Behavioral tests for overwrite protection, argument completeness, frozen
  empirical protocols, evidence anchors, and live venue provenance.
- English and Chinese v2 documentation, safe examples, sourcing policy, and
  transfer instructions.

### Removed

- Universal quotas for hypotheses, repetitions, ablations, environments, and
  reviewer agreement.
- Silent third-party skill installation and unreviewed install-hook execution.
- Claims that test counts, verbosity, or simulated reviews predict acceptance.
